Documentation

Everything in the game as it's built now. Each floor's details stay hidden until you open that floor.

The run

  • Pokémon from outside can't be used. You begin the descent with an Egg found in the Veil. It hatches into Chikorita, Tepig or Sobble, picked at random each run, as their Veil variants (below).
  • One catch per area. Each upper and lower section, and each named place, allows one catch. Dupes clause: a Pokémon from an evolution line you already own doesn't use it up. While the catch is unused, the game re-rolls a duplicate line up to 8 times. Unown are the exception: no catch limit, no dupes clause.
  • Fainted means gone. A Pokémon that faints is lost for the rest of the run.
  • Losing ends the run. A lost battle wipes the run and you land back at the start of the Veil with a fresh team. Some things carry over between runs.
  • Every Pokémon obeys you, whatever its level.

Levels and stats

  • Level caps. EXP stops at the cap. The cap rises after each floor's boss: .
  • The Candy Jar takes a Pokémon straight to the current cap. It learns its moves and evolves on the way, and the jar never runs out. There are no Rare Candies in the game.
  • No EVs. Effort values are off for everyone. Stats come from level, base stats, IVs and nature.
  • Trainers have perfect IVs (31 in every stat) on both difficulties.

Normal and Hard

You choose a difficulty at the start of the game. Both use the same rebuilt battle AI: it reads your team, switches smartly and won't throw away its moves. Hard changes the teams:

  • one more Pokémon per team (up to six), chosen for the team's plan;
  • held items on almost every Pokémon. On Floors 1-2, only the floor bosses hold Life Orb, Choice items, Leftovers, Black Sludge, Expert Belt or Assault Vest;
  • stronger moves (to 80 power on Floor 1, more on later floors), never trading away a move a Pokémon's ability relies on;
  • the ace (the last Pokémon) a level or two higher.

The Trainers tab shows both versions of every team.

Out of battle

  • Running Shoes from the start; the Portable PC once you enter the Veil. L opens the Key Items menu.
  • The Smoke Ball always gets you away from a wild battle.
  • Dig and Teleport don't work as escape moves outside battle. Berries can't be planted.
