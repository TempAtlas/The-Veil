The Veil

Beneath the region lies the Veil: a vast underground expanse that no expedition has ever mapped to the bottom. Most who go in never return. The ones who do come back changed. Delvers say the Veil never ends, and that Pokémon who died on the surface have been seen alive inside it.

One rule holds down there: Pokémon brought in from outside cannot be used. Your Champion team stays at the gate. Everything you fight with, you find in the dark.

How it plays

  • A descent, floor by floor. Each floor is its own place with its own rules: a fractured cave system, a drowned city, and stranger things below.
  • Runs. Lose a battle and the run is over: you start again at the top with a fresh team. Each run starts a little differently, and the Veil remembers some of what happened.
  • One catch per area. Every section allows a single catch. A Pokémon from a line you already own doesn't use it up.
  • Level caps and no grinding. Each floor has a level cap. The Candy Jar raises a Pokémon straight to it, so your time goes on routing and choices, not on grinding.
  • Trainers with a plan. Every trainer's team is built around one idea, and they use it well. Choose Normal or Hard at the start. Hard brings bigger teams, held items and sharper sets.
  • Things you aren't meant to fight. Between the floors you'll meet some of these. Run.

Where it stands

The game is in active development and not released yet. The docs, the map and the damage calculator follow the current build. The patcher will open at launch.
